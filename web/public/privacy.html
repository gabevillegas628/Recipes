Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Gabe Cooks (gabecooks.com) is a private recipe box and family organizer used by one household. Accounts are created by the site owner; there is no public sign-up.

What the app stores

  • Your name, email address and a hashed password, to sign you in.
  • What you save: recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, notes, reminders and appointments, and any photos you add.

This is stored on the app's server and database, hosted by Railway. It is visible only to the people with an account on this site.

AI processing

When you import a recipe, sort groceries, or add something on the Add screen, the text or photo you provide is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to read and organize it. Anthropic processes it to return a result and does not use it to train its models. Data from Google is never sent to Anthropic.

Google Calendar

If someone in the household connects a Google account on the Settings page, the app:

  • reads the list of calendars that account can edit, so you can choose which one to use;
  • creates, updates and deletes events on that one calendar for the appointments and reminders saved in this app.

The app does not read your other events, contacts or email. It stores the Google account's email address, the chosen calendar, the IDs of the events it created, and an encrypted access token so it can keep the calendar up to date. Disconnecting on the Settings page deletes the token and revokes the app's access; you can also revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Gabe Cooks' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else. The only outside services involved are the hosting provider (Railway), Anthropic for the AI features above, and Google Calendar if you connect it.

Deleting your data

You can delete anything you've saved from within the app. To delete your account and everything in it, ask the site owner.
