Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Mise (gabecooks.com) is a private family organizer and recipe box used by one household. Accounts are created by the site owner; there is no public sign-up.

What the app stores

  • Your name, email address and a hashed password, to sign you in.
  • What you save: recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, notes, reminders and appointments, and any photos you add.

This is stored on the app's server and database, hosted by Railway. It is visible only to the people with an account on this site.

AI processing

When you import a recipe, sort groceries, or add something on the Add screen, the text or photo you provide is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to read and organize it. Anthropic processes it to return a result and does not use it to train its models.

When you ask the app to find a time, the titles of events on the family calendar are also sent to Anthropic, only to work out which family member each event is for. Nothing else from Google is sent to Anthropic.

Google Calendar

If someone in the household connects a Google account on the Settings page, the app:

  • reads the list of calendars that account can edit, so you can choose which ones to use;
  • reads the events on the chosen family calendar, to show the family's schedule on the Today screen and to find open times for new appointments;
  • creates, updates and deletes events for the appointments and reminders saved in this app, on the calendars you chose.

The app does not read other calendars, contacts or email. It stores the Google account's email address, the chosen calendars, the IDs of the events it created, which family member each event title refers to, and an encrypted access token so it can keep the calendar up to date. Disconnecting on the Settings page deletes the token and revokes the app's access; you can also revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Mise's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else. The only outside services involved are the hosting provider (Railway), Anthropic for the AI features above, and Google Calendar if you connect it.

Deleting your data

You can delete anything you've saved from within the app. To delete your account and everything in it, ask the site owner.
